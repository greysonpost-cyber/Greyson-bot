INSTALL
1. Back up your project/database first.
2. Upload the included src folder and replace the matching file.
3. Do NOT replace your data folder, bot.sqlite, .env, or Railway volume.
4. Redeploy the bot.

FIXES
- Restores a detailed giveaway embed with prize, exact end time, relative timer,
  winner count, entry count, host, requirements, bonus-entry roles, and DRIP tag rule.
- Ensures newly created giveaway tickets receive a claim panel and winner ping.
- Adds a fallback ticket message if the normal claim panel fails to send.
- Auto-claim tickets now also receive a visible winner notification.

This patch does not contain configuration or database files.
