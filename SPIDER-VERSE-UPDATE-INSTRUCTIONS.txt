NEW COMMANDS

  - Adds +1 entry, once every 24 hours.

/passive web-swing
  - Owner of Web Swing claims +1 Power Token, once every 24 hours.

/passive status
  - Shows ownership, cooldowns, and Friendly Neighborhood refund usage.
