WHAT CHANGED
- /tokens daily now grants exactly 1 PT every 24 hours.
- /passive web-slinger privately claims +1 PT every 24 hours for owners of Web Swing or Web Slinger.
- Tournament rounds now open a private power-selection phase before each game.
- Shield costs 10 PT and blocks one removal/elimination during that round.
- Double Points costs 5 PT and doubles points awarded in that round.
- Only one power may be chosen per round, and selection locks when a manager uses /tournament begin-round.
- Tournament finish rewards are automatic: 1st 15 PT, 2nd 10 PT, 3rd 5 PT.
- Giveaway winners are announced, but prizes are fulfilled manually; no automatic claim ticket is created.
- Server minigame/event winners receive a random 1-5 PT, subject to the existing daily minigame cap.

ROUND FLOW
1. Manager uses /tournament start (or /tournament next-round).
2. Players pick one power from the tournament panel or /tournament power.
3. Manager uses /tournament begin-round to lock selections and start the game.
