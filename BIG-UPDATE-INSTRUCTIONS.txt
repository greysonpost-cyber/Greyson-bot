IMPORTANT SETUP COMMANDS:
/config set-category category:<your ticket category>
/config set-value key:giveaway_claim_category value:<CATEGORY_ID>
/config set-giveaway-role type:Giveaway Staff role:<role>
/config set-giveaway-role type:Host Role role:<role>
/config set-giveaway-role type:Lock Role role:<role>
/config set-giveaway-role type:Unlock Role role:<role>

GIVEAWAY PRESET EXAMPLE:
/giveaway preset-save name:standard default_claim_minutes:10 bonus_role:@Booster bonus_entries:2 claim_bonus_role:@VIP claim_bonus_minutes:30 auto_claim_role:@AutoClaim ticket_category:<category>

Then start one with:
/giveaway start prize:Dragonfly duration:1h preset:standard

Railway will register slash commands automatically during startup.
