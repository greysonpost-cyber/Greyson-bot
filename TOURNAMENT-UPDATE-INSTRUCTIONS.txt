DRIPCORE v2.3.2 — BUTTON-BASED DTI SCREENSHOT SUBMISSIONS

UPLOAD
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Upload the included src folder into the ROOT of your GitHub repository.
3. Replace matching files.
4. Do NOT replace/delete data/, bot.sqlite, .env, or your Railway Volume.
5. Commit and let Railway redeploy so the updated slash command registers.

WHAT THIS UPDATE CHANGES
- Players no longer use /tournament submit-outfit.
- /tournament open-dti posts a Submit Outfit button and a View Rules button.
- Clicking Submit Outfit opens a private DM workflow with DripCore.
- The player sends one image attachment in DMs, keeping the entry anonymous.
- The message text can be used as an optional caption.
- Clicking Submit Outfit again before the deadline replaces the previous entry.
- The database deadline is checked again when the DM screenshot arrives. Late screenshots are rejected even if the button message still exists.
- The DM collection request lasts up to five minutes and never beyond the actual tournament deadline.

DRESS TO IMPRESS FLOW
1. Advance the tournament to Round 2.
2. A manager opens submissions:
   /tournament open-dti theme:Summer Vacation minutes:10
3. The bot posts a public Submit Outfit button.
4. A participant clicks the button and receives a DM from DripCore.
5. The participant sends one screenshot in that DM. Optional caption text may be included in the same message.
6. After the exact deadline, a manager opens anonymous voting:
   /tournament open-dti-voting
7. Every active participant secretly votes:
   /tournament vote-dti submission:<number>
8. Close after everyone voted:
   /tournament close-dti-voting
9. Emergency manager override:
   /tournament close-dti-voting force:true

IMPORTANT
- The bot must be able to DM participants. Users with closed DMs will be told to enable direct messages and click again.
- Screenshot entries are collected in DMs and are not posted in the tournament channel until the anonymous gallery opens.
- Vote choices are stored in SQLite but never shown publicly.
- Only final totals and placements are revealed; individual votes stay private.
- Only Tournament Managers/admins can report MM2 winners and losers.
